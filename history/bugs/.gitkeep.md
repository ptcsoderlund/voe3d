# bugs/archive

**Reports that are no longer anybody's to decide.** A report lands here the moment it
becomes work — one or more cards exist in `todo/` naming it — or is finished without
work (`closed`, `not a bug`). Its number, filename and evidence travel with it.

**It is not a graveyard.** If the card that consumed a report is abandoned without
fixing it, the report goes back to `bugs/` with its status reset and a dated line
saying what happened.

**It is the project's only real defect history** — cards record work, not faults — so
it is kept rather than tidied.

Rules: ADR-0110, amending ADR-0108.
