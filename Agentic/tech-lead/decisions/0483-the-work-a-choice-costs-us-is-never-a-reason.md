# 0483 — The work a choice costs us is never a reason
date: 2026-10-28
by: tech-lead

## Decision
How much work a choice is to build is never a reason for or against it, for the tech-lead or the planner.
We choose what is best for the product in the long run: speed, quality and what it lets us do later. A
large shader, a new subsystem, a rewrite or many cards are no argument against an option. Risk, what it
costs at run time, and how it fits the givens (0168) still count. A decision's Reasoning never names "less
work", "smaller" or "simpler to build" as the reason it wins.

## Reasoning
The sponsor's call on 2026-10-28, after the CPU texture encoder 0477 chose as "the smallest encoder"
made the first open of a small project take more than 15 minutes. Weighing the work an option costs is
how that happened. Alternatives: keep effort as a tie-breaker (rejected: it is how 0477 was decided).

## Replaces
nothing. It applies to every decision from now on, and is grounds to reopen an older one whose reasoning
rested on it.
