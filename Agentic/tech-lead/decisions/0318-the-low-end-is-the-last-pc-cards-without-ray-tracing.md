# 0318 — The low end is the last PC cards without ray tracing, judged by the sponsor
date: 2026-10-02
by: tech-lead

## Decision
VOE3D's main target is the PC. The low end every feature must run well on is the last generation of PC
graphics cards without hardware ray tracing, the cards just before the PS5 (a GTX 1060 to 1660, an RX 5700
class). Performance is not measured by tests or budgets in work orders: the sponsor reviews and tests on
their own machines, laptops held back on power, and raises a feature that keeps them from relaxing as a
problem. A card still proves what it changes, as before; it does not time it.

## Reasoning
The sponsor's call (2026-10-02): PC first, low end before ray tracing, judged by use.
- Integrated graphics or a Steam Deck as the low end: a smaller look than a PC game needs.
- A time budget in every work order: numbers no one plays by, and a machine the sponsor would have to keep.

## Replaces
Nothing. Gives 0316's "as many people as possible" its floor.
