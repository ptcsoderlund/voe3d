# 02 — Enemy tanks break without being shot

## Seen
"The enemy tanks randomly break, even if I am not shooting."

## Expected
An enemy tank turns into its wreck only when a shell the player fired hits it. Enemy shells never
wreck an enemy tank: one that hits another enemy stops on it and nothing changes, the same as a
shell hitting a wreck. No enemy breaks while the player holds fire.

## How to reproduce
1. Open the tank game in the editor and press Play.
2. Do not fire. Drive near the enemies, or just wait, and let them shoot.
3. Watch the enemy tanks: after a while some of them turn into wrecks.
