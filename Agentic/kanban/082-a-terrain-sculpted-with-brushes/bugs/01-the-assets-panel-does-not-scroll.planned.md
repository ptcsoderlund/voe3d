# 01 — The Assets panel does not scroll

## Seen
The Assets panel has no scroll. When its rows are taller than the panel, the rows past the bottom edge cannot be
reached.

## Expected
When the rows don't fit, the Assets panel scrolls (mouse wheel, and a scroll bar like the editor's other
scrolling panels), so every row can be reached, selected, renamed and dragged.

## How to reproduce
1. Open a project with more assets than fit in the Assets panel's height, or make the panel short.
2. Try to scroll the Assets panel with the mouse wheel.
3. The list does not move; the rows below the panel's edge stay out of reach.
