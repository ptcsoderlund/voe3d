# coder 108-05

## Learned
- Every Inspector section now draws on the SURFACE colour with a border (ui's section box). The RAISED shade the component, Sculpt, Layers, Foliage, Brush and LOD panels used to have is gone.
- While Layers or Foliage is folded, none of its rows is recorded, so its rectangles stay zero and dropping a material or `.glb` onto that section lands nowhere until it is opened again.
- `VOE_EDITOR_INSPECTOR_LEVEL_NODES` and `_ELEMENTS` now count the section's four nodes and five elements. Before, they counted a panel's.

## Assumed
- A component section's box is keyed "component" plus `type.value`, not the loop index. The two are the same number, because types are numbered 0 to count in registration order.
- The budget in interface.h counts all four nodes and five elements of each of the 45 sections on top of the old total, even though the box and header row replace a panel and row already counted. Over-counting seemed safer than finding out exactly how much slack there was.

## Surprised
- The contents of the Sculpt, Layers, Foliage and Brush sections were never counted in interface.h's node or element budget. Only LOD had a paragraph there.
