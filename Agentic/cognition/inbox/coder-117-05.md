# coder 117-05

## Learned
- A species entry passes foliage's kind filter as is (loaded, partless, no picture, material or landscape), so the kind lookup stays once per kind; a copy whose variation is not loaded yet is neither drawn nor counted, as a kind not loaded.

## Assumed
- The per-row slot table was keyed by kind; variations made it keyed by drawn entry, with offsets into pools as large as the old per-kind tables (sixteen whole `.glb`s). A row past that room leaves the copy out and raises the existing left-out message, reworded to name both causes. Keyed by (kind, variation) whole it would have been about 290 KB of stack.
- Added case 16 to `3d/tests/draw_foliage.c` (a species over two `.glb`s loaded at `KIND#v`); the test's device capacities were widened to hold the second model.
