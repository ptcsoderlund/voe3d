# coder 115-09

## Assumed
- `voe_editor_models_landscape_take` counts a path as held when the store has any entry for it, a failed one included, so a broken landscape fixed on disk can be taken in over its failed entry.
- A taken landscape loads at its entry's existing stamp: the store never re-reads a landscape by stamp (game/models.c), so the stamp carries no meaning for it.
- Several landscapes can wait at once, at most 8 (one arena each); a ninth in the same update is left out with a stderr line, as a full material reload wait is.
- `voe_editor_project_scene_take` names the open prefab's path, else `file.scene`, when the strict check refuses; a refusal from `_scene_set` after the check passed keeps that call's "the scene" subject.
- A taken project file's changed scene path lives in a new `file_arena` on the project, made on the first take that changes the path and cleared at each.
