# coder 108-04

## Learned
- "kind 15 " in the fold name's room is because a landscape holds at most `VOE_ASSETS_LANDSCAPE_KINDS` (16) kinds, so `voe_editor_folds_kind_name` asserts `kind < 16`. `VOE_EDITOR_FOLD_NAME` comes to 136 bytes.

## Assumed
- When the store is full (128 names), a fold is dropped and nothing is reported, so the section stays open. Past 64 buttons in one frame, a section still draws but its button is not recorded. Both limits are named in folds.h's header. The card only spelled out the load-time drop.
- A failed fold write is reported only through `VOE_BASE_ERROR` at the site, not as the top bar's notice, because `voe_editor_folds_read` and `_clear` return void on the card. Snap's write turns a failure into a notice in interface.c; folds do not.
- `fold` lines are read with a trailing `\r` cut, as settings.c's number reads tolerate one.
