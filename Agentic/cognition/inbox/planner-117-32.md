## Assumed
- A branch whose first segment never leaves the trunk keeps today's joint; with 0519's sizes this is only very short, thick branches near the top.
- The trunk joint lives in the mesh step alone; the skeleton still starts a branch on the trunk's axis, so wind and chopping later see an unbent branch.

## Learned
- The grower has three orders, so "branches on branches" in 0518 are this grower's twigs (order 2): a trunk joint is exactly an order-1 limb's joint.
- Far trunk levels have 6 and 3 sides; a point on the round trunk floats up to half the radius off a 3-sided trunk, so anything placed on the trunk must follow that level's facets.
