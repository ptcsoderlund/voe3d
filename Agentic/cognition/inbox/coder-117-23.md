# coder 117-23

## Learned
- Confirmed in `place_cards`: a card's `along` axis is its twig segment's direction and `across` points out from the twig (the centre sits `across` × half the out edge off the twig), so `leaf_size` is the length along the twig and `leaf_width` × `leaf_size` the edge out from it.
- A card's uvs carry its axes: u runs out from the twig, v along it, whichever way the quad is wound to face off the crown.
