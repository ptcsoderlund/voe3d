# coder 115-10

## Assumed
- `voe_editor_file_take` asserts on the open prefab, a `.scene`, a `.landscape` or any other kind: card 12 routes those elsewhere, so reaching it with one is the caller's bug.
- A file step side of no bytes is not written (platform asserts on a write of no bytes): `_apply` says "left as it is, that side empty" in `why` and returns false.
- A landscape step keeps both copies in one malloced arena of its own (1 MB blocks), not one per side.

## Surprised
- A `.material` taken in reads the tables again but does not touch the Inspector's open copy (`scene->material`), which `voe_editor_file_take` cannot reach; the caller that holds the scene has to refresh it.
