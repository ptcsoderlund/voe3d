# coder 108-03

## Learned
- A section's box cannot grow to its parent's width on its own: layout asserts on GROW across, so a column child is widened only by its parent's `across = FILL`. The box sets FILL on its own children, so nested sections and body rows take its width; a top-level section takes a panel's width only if that panel FILLs.
- The fold button is keyed "fold"/0 inside the box, so a title choice composed into the header row may not be named "fold".

## Assumed
- Box pad 2, gap 1.5 and header-row gap 2 millimetres at spacing one, matching the editor's SECTION_PAD and SECTION_GAP, so swapping an Inspector panel for a section keeps its look.
- The header row centres the fold button and title across (vertically), with no pad of its own.

## Surprised
- The card says the box "grows to its parent's width"; layout has no way for a child to ask that across the flow, so it depends on the caller's parent FILLing, as the existing Inspector panels already do.
