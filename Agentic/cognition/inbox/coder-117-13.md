# coder 117-13

## Assumed
- A take sets the species world from a recorded text whose tree row may wear another variation than the
  stepper shows (stepping a variation is no edit, so no state records it). So undo.c, after applying a
  tree step, clamps `tree_variation` and steps the species to it again. The card did not ask for this;
  without it Ctrl+Z could show variation 0 while the Inspector says another.
