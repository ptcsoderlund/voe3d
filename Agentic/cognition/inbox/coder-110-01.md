# coder 110-01

## Learned
- The guarantee that a nested burst gets workers only holds while the outer bursts whose tasks wait on nested bursts are no wider in total than standing − 1. Two such splash-wide bursts at once could fill every standing worker with waiting tasks and hang. One outer burst at a time, as the splash wait is, is safe.

## Assumed
- `_finish` waits until every task has ended and also until no worker is still on the burst. A worker reads the burst's next index once more after its last task, so freeing as soon as `ended == count` could free memory a worker is still using.
- A worker's claim on a burst (`room`) is never handed back. A worker leaves a burst only when no indices are left, so a returned place could never be used.
- `voe_platform_workers_close` must not run while another thread starts a burst. That is stated as a constraint in the `burst.c` header and was not built in. 0504 has the app close the workers last, in `voe_app_destroy`.
- `_start` calls open every time instead of reading the standing count first. Open does nothing while workers stand, so the result is the same with one less lock round trip.
