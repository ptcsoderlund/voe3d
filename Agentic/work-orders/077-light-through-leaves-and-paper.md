# 077 — Light through leaves and paper

## What
A material can let light through diffusely (diffuse transmission): a thin leaf, paper or a porcelain cup
glows on its shaded side when a light or the bright sky is behind it, in its own colour. Nothing behind it
is seen through it; that is glass (076). It costs little.

## Why
Backlit leaves are what makes plants read as plants, cheaply.

## Models
From https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models: DiffuseTransmissionTest, DiffuseTransmissionPlant, DiffuseTransmissionTeacup, MandarinOrange.

## How to test
1. Place DiffuseTransmissionTest. Open it in the Khronos glTF Sample Viewer (https://github.khronos.org/glTF-Sample-Viewer-Release/). Each panel glows from behind as there.
2. Place DiffuseTransmissionPlant and put a point light behind it. The leaves glow on the side facing you.
   Move the light to the front: the glow goes and the leaves are lit normally.
3. Place DiffuseTransmissionTeacup and MandarinOrange. At a glance they look like the viewer.
4. Press Play: the same in the game window.
