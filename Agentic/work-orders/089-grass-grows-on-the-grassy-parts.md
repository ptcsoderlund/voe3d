# 089 — Grass grows on the grassy parts

## What
Grass blades grow wherever the grass layer (084) is painted, thick where it is strong and thin where it
fades out. Repainting the layer changes the grass at once. The grass fades out with distance, and the far
slopes keep their green from the ground's own colour, so there is no hard line where the grass ends.
Grass is lit and shadowed like the rest. A field of it stays smooth to fly over.

## Why
Grass on the slopes is what makes the hill feel alive, and 0374 asks for it on the grassy parts.

## How to test
1. Open the painted hill. Grass stands where I painted grass, and none on dirt, moss or rock.
2. Paint grass into a new patch. Blades grow there as I paint. Erase some: they go.
3. Fly from up close to far away. The grass thins out softly, with no line where it stops.
4. Turn the sun. The grass is lit and shadowed by trees and the house.
5. Fly low over a big field on my laptop. It stays smooth. The frame breakdown names the grass's share.
6. Press Play, and ship: the same grass in both.
