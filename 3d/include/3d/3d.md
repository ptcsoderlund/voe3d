# 3d

`3d`'s public headers: the components that name what an entity draws, the system
that turns those tables into draws, the matrices and the sort a draw needs, and
the import that fills a world from a file. Nothing here names Vulkan.

- `projection.h` — a field of view into a projection matrix. Its header says why
  this is neither `math`'s nor `scene`'s, and which three files have to agree
  about depth running backwards.
- `mesh_component.h` — what an entity draws, as a range in `render`'s pools, and
  which of the two layers it draws in. Its header says why it holds an id and not
  a buffer, why the layer is a property of the drawable rather than of the
  material or the transform, why neither layer is the normal case, why the table
  is written directly rather than through a system and what would change that,
  and why the geometry is the one field that changes after creation.
- `panel_component.h` — what an entity draws when it draws a surface of elements:
  a range of this frame's element buffer, the surface's size in millimetres and
  which layer it is in. Its header says why the range is one frame's and wrong
  the moment that frame has ended, why a panel is a component and not an entity,
  why it holds no matrix and which four factors the draw system composes instead,
  why the size is here rather than read off the elements, that a millimetre on a
  panel is a real millimetre and where the other meaning of the word lives, why
  there is no panel system, and why the range is the only field that changes
  after creation.
- `material_component.h` — the factors and texture ids a surface is shaded with,
  its alpha mode, whether it is lit at all, whether its base colour texture is a
  distance field rather than a picture, which rectangle of that texture it reads,
  and the record `render` holds for them. Its header says which four channels are
  shaded with and which two are only stored, where the deduplication of a shared
  picture comes from, why the alpha mode is `render`'s enum rather than a second
  one, why neither the unlit flag nor the distance-field one is something the
  importer ever sets, why the rectangle is the base colour's alone, and why a
  scale of nothing in it means the whole texture.
- `shape_component.h` — which built-in shape an entity draws, as one read-only,
  described number a saved scene keeps. Its header says why a shape and not a
  mesh or material is what survives a save, why kind names one of a small fixed
  set rather than an arbitrary asset, and why kind is read-only.
- `shape_system.h` — the shapes' GPU side — one cube's geometry and the one grey
  material every shape wears — and the run that gives a mesh and a material to a
  shape that has neither yet. Its header says why the upload is a startup
  operation, what the capacity constants size a device with, why the run is
  idempotent, why the world must already have mesh and material registered, and
  how an unknown kind is reported.
- `normal_matrix.h` — a world matrix into the matrix its normals want. Its header
  says why a normal cannot be carried by the world matrix, what that looks like
  on screen, and why this is neither `math`'s nor `render`'s.
- `depth_sort.h` — the order see-through objects have to be drawn in. Its header
  says why blending is not commutative, why the key is one point per object, and
  which way round "further away" is.
- `draw_system.h` — the world drawn into the pass the loop has opened, and the
  camera and sun worked out once for it. Its header says why the loop owns the
  frame and how its phases are ordered, why the camera is computed before the
  pass and handed back, why a begin with nothing to draw into is the loop's case,
  why the run returns nothing, what the draw loop deliberately does not do, which
  pass an entity's alpha mode puts it in and why table order is safe for one of
  them and not the other, why a pass may leave one entity out and why that is one
  entity and not a list, how the two layers run across those two passes with one
  depth clear between them, why the layer is a clear rather than the depth test
  turned off, why it decides order and never lighting, which three components an
  entity needs before it is drawn, why it wants an arena, and why a world needs
  exactly one camera and exactly one sun.
- `import.h` — a `.glb`'s bytes into entities. Its header says which folder each
  step of the pipeline belongs to, where the node tree is flattened, and the one
  thing decomposing a world matrix loses.
