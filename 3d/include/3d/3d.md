# 3d

The public headers, one entry each; the fuller account of every one of them, and
the questions each answers, stays on `3d/3d.md`.

- `projection.h` — a field of view, or the sun's box, into a projection matrix,
  with depth running backwards.
- `shadow_cascades.h` — the sun's four shadow cascades fitted to one view: the
  record a camera pass reads and the light views the shadow passes draw with.
- `bounce_grid.h` — the bounce probe grid fitted to one view: its whole cells
  about the world origin, its corner about the eye and the bounce pass's light view.
- `mesh_component.h` — what an entity draws, as a range in `render`'s pools, and
  which of the two layers it draws in.
- `panel_component.h` — what an entity draws when it draws a surface of
  elements: this frame's range of element records, the surface's size in
  millimetres, and its layer.
- `material_component.h` — the factors, texture ids, alpha mode and two flags a
  surface is shaded with, and the record `render` holds for them.
- `shape_component.h` — which built-in shape an entity draws, as one read-only,
  described number a saved scene keeps.
- `shape_system.h` — the shapes' GPU side, and the run that gives a mesh and a
  material to a shape that has neither yet.
- `shape_geometry.h` — the built-in shapes' triangles, and the edges of each
  surface with the two normals that meet along them, as the CPU sees them.
- `pick.h` — the ray a view's camera sends through a point of its picture, and
  the entity that ray meets first.
- `outline.h` — one entity's silhouette, seen from one camera, as the quads a
  selection outline is drawn from.
- `camera_marker.h` — a scene camera drawn in an editor's view as its box and
  frustum in line quads, and the ray that picks the box.
- `collider_marker.h` — a collider drawn in an editor's view as line quads about
  the eye (box edges, sphere circles, capsule outline), built in an arena from
  physics's world shape.
- `sun_marker.h` — a sun drawn in an editor's view as a circle and an arrow the
  way it shines, in line quads about the eye, and the ray that picks it.
- `gizmo.h` — the move gizmo as arithmetic: its size at the distance it stands,
  the handle a ray meets, the point a drag is measured from, and its triangles.
- `gizmo_rings.h` — the rotate gizmo as arithmetic: three rings about the world's
  axes, the ring a ray meets, the angle about its axis a ray points at, and its
  triangles.
- `normal_matrix.h` — a world matrix into the matrix its normals want.
- `depth_sort.h` — the order see-through objects have to be drawn in.
- `draw_system.h` — the sun's shadow passes, then the world drawn into the pass
  the loop has opened, with the camera and the sun worked out once for it; each
  live particle one blended draw.
- `import.h` — a `.glb`'s bytes into entities.
- `model_component.h` — which model file an entity wears, as one path relative
  to the project folder that a saved scene keeps, and the intent that changes it.
- `emitter_component.h` — a particle emitter as a described row a saved scene
  keeps, its replace and play/stop/burst intents, and its runtime-only particles.
- `emitter_system.h` — the run that drains an emitter's intents, adds and drops
  its particles row, and spawns, moves and kills its particles.
- `water_component.h` — a body of water as a described row a saved scene keeps,
  its replace intent, and the runtime-only clock its waves run on.
- `water_system.h` — the run that drains a water's replaces, adds and drops its
  waves row, and steps its clock, wrapped below 60 s.
- `models.h` — the store of loaded models, keyed by path: a file loaded once,
  loaded again when it changes, kept as failed when it fails, cleared for another
  project, and walked by drawing, picking and outlines; pictures and the soft dot too.
