# 3d

The 3D renderer: the components that name what an entity draws — a mesh, a
surface of elements or a built-in shape — the system that turns those tables
into draws with a camera and a sun, the two matrices an object is drawn with,
the order see-through things have to be drawn in, and the step that reads a
model into a world. The only folder that may name `render`, `scene`,
`ecs` and `assets` at once, which is why the import lives here.

Nothing here names Vulkan. A mesh is an id, a material is factors and ids, and
a gap in what `render` can do is filled in `render` — never reached around.

- `include` — the public headers, in `include/3d/`; each is listed below by path.
- `src` — the implementation: the component tables, the two systems, the two
  matrices, the sort and the import; each file is listed on `src/src.md`.
- `tests` — one plain C program per module, found by the build; each is listed
  on `tests/tests.md`.
- `include/3d/projection.h` — a field of view into a projection matrix. Its
  header says why this is neither `math`'s nor `scene`'s, and which three files
  have to agree about depth running backwards.
- `include/3d/mesh_component.h` — what an entity draws, as a range in `render`'s
  pools, and which of the two layers it draws in. Its header says why it holds an
  id and not a buffer, why the layer is a property of the drawable rather than of
  the material or the transform, why neither layer is the normal case, why the
  table is written directly rather than through a system and what would change
  that, and why the geometry is the one field that changes after creation.
- `include/3d/panel_component.h` — what an entity draws when it draws a surface
  of elements: a range of this frame's element buffer, the surface's size in
  millimetres and which layer it is in. Its header says why the range is one
  frame's and wrong the moment that frame has ended, why a panel is a component
  and not an entity, why it holds no matrix and which four factors the draw
  system composes instead, why the size is here rather than read off the
  elements, that a millimetre on a panel is a real millimetre and where the other
  meaning of the word lives, why there is no panel system, and why the range is
  the only field that changes after creation.
- `include/3d/material_component.h` — the factors and texture ids a surface is
  shaded with, its alpha mode, whether it is lit at all, whether its base colour
  texture is a distance field rather than a picture, which rectangle of that
  texture it reads, and the record `render` holds for them. Its header says which
  four channels are shaded with and which two are only stored, where the
  deduplication of a shared picture comes from, why the alpha mode is `render`'s
  enum rather than a second one, why neither the unlit flag nor the
  distance-field one is something the importer ever sets, why the rectangle is
  the base colour's alone, and why a scale of nothing in it means the whole
  texture.
- `include/3d/shape_component.h` — which built-in shape an entity draws and in
  what colour, as a kind chosen from its three names and a linear colour a
  saved scene keeps. Its header says why a shape and not a mesh or material is
  what survives a save, why kind names one of a small fixed set rather than an
  arbitrary asset, why the kinds are named and numbered from one, why the
  colour is linear and edited through the intent, and why transforms are
  registered first.
- `include/3d/shape_system.h` — the shapes' GPU side — their geometry and the
  one white material every shape wears — the shape's intent, and the run that
  drains it, gives a mesh and a material to a shape that has neither yet and
  drops both once the shape is gone. Its header says why the upload is a
  startup operation, what the capacity constants size a device with, why the
  run is idempotent, how an intent is corrected, why the world must already
  have mesh and material registered and needs a structural queue, why a removed
  shape draws one more frame, and how an unknown kind is reported.
- `include/3d/shape_geometry.h` — the three built-in shapes' triangles and their
  surfaces' edges, kept on the CPU and keyed by kind. Its header says why a ray
  and a silhouette need geometry the card cannot be asked for, why it is built
  from the same three builders the upload uses rather than written out a second
  time, why the weld is by position and what its tolerance is, what a closed
  surface leaves every edge with, why it is keyed by kind, and that the arena is
  the caller's.
- `include/3d/pick.h` — what is under this pixel: the ray a view's camera sends
  through a point of its picture, and the frontmost entity that ray meets. Its
  header says why the question is this folder's and not the editor's, why the
  shape table is the whole of what a project can be clicked on today, how a hit
  is measured and why the direction is not normalised again in the shape's own
  space, which entities are skipped and why, that ties go to table order, and
  why every row is walked every call.
- `include/3d/normal_matrix.h` — a world matrix into the matrix its normals
  want. Its header says why a normal cannot be carried by the world matrix, what
  that looks like on screen, and why this is neither `math`'s nor `render`'s.
- `include/3d/depth_sort.h` — the order see-through objects have to be drawn
  in. Its header says why blending is not commutative, why the key is one point
  per object, and which way round "further away" is.
- `include/3d/draw_system.h` — the world drawn into the pass the loop has
  opened, and the camera and sun worked out once for it. Its header says why
  the loop owns the frame and how its phases are ordered, why the camera is
  computed before the pass and handed back, why a begin with nothing to draw
  into is the loop's case, why the run returns nothing, what the draw loop
  deliberately does not do, which pass an entity's alpha mode puts it in and
  why table order is safe for one of them and not the other, why a pass may
  leave one entity out and why that is one entity and not a list, how the two
  layers run across those two passes with one depth clear between them, why the
  layer is a clear rather than the depth test turned off, why it decides order
  and never lighting, which three components an entity needs before it is
  drawn, where a drawn object's colour comes from, why it wants an arena, and
  why a world needs exactly one camera and exactly one sun.
- `include/3d/import.h` — a `.glb`'s bytes into entities. Its header says which
  folder each step of the pipeline belongs to, where the node tree is flattened,
  and the one thing decomposing a world matrix loses.
