# 3d

The 3D renderer: the components that name GPU resources, the system that turns
the tables into draws with a camera and a sun, the two matrices an object is
drawn with, and the step that reads a model into a world. The only folder that
may name `render`, `scene`, `ecs` and `assets` at once, which is why the import
lives here.

Nothing here names Vulkan. A mesh is an id, a material is factors and ids, and
a gap in what `render` can do is filled in `render` — never reached around.

- `include/3d/projection.h` — a field of view into a projection matrix. Its
  header says why this is neither `math`'s nor `scene`'s, and which three files
  have to agree about depth running backwards.
- `include/3d/mesh_component.h` — what an entity draws, as a range in `render`'s
  pools. Its header says why it holds an id and not a buffer, and why there is
  no mesh system yet.
- `include/3d/material_component.h` — the factors and texture ids a surface is
  shaded with, and the record `render` holds for them. Its header says which
  four channels are shaded with and which two are only stored, and where the
  deduplication of a shared picture comes from.
- `include/3d/normal_matrix.h` — a world matrix into the matrix its normals
  want. Its header says why a normal cannot be carried by the world matrix, what
  that looks like on screen, and why this is neither `math`'s nor `render`'s.
- `include/3d/draw_system.h` — one frame, one call. Its header says what this
  card's draw loop deliberately does not do, why table order is safe, which
  three components an entity needs before it is drawn, and why a world needs
  exactly one camera and exactly one sun.
- `include/3d/import.h` — a `.glb`'s bytes into entities. Its header says which
  folder each step of the pipeline belongs to, where the node tree is flattened,
  and the one thing decomposing a world matrix loses.
- `src/projection.c` — the arithmetic, written out, because the signs are the
  whole thing.
- `src/normal_matrix.c` — the inverse transpose, the derivation in three lines,
  and the one branch a flattened object needs.
- `src/mesh_component.c` — the key, the creation call and the reads.
- `src/material_component.c` — the same, plus the upload that turns a
  material's numbers into a record.
- `src/draw_system.c` — the camera, the sun, two matrices per object, and one
  draw per mesh.
- `src/import.c` — the uploads and the tree walk. Its header says why the order
  is forced, why uploading the pictures has to read the materials first, why the
  walk needs no recursion, and why nothing unwinds on failure.
- `tests/projection.c` — reversed depth, the aspect ratio, and that nothing in
  the projection flips Y. Needs no graphics card.
- `tests/normal_matrix.c` — that a normal stays perpendicular to a
  non-uniformly scaled surface and that the world matrix's answer does not. Its
  header says which three cases would pass with the wrong matrix. Needs no
  graphics card.
- `tests/import.c` — a hand-built `.glb` in and entities out: the flattening,
  the shared picture, and one frame drawn from what it made. Its header says
  what every number in the file is for and that the mirrored model is what it is
  really looking for.
- `tests/model_data.inc` — that file, as bytes.
