# 3d

The 3D renderer: the components that name what an entity draws, the system that
turns those tables into draws with a camera and a sun, the matrices and the
order a draw needs, the shapes' own triangles a ray and a silhouette are walked
over, the three gizmos, and the step that reads a model into a world. Nothing here
names Vulkan, and a gap in what `render` can do is filled in `render`.

- `include` — the public headers, in `include/3d/`; each is listed below by path.
- `src` — the implementation: the component tables, the two systems, the two
  matrices, the sort, the shapes' own triangles, the ray, the silhouette, the
  gizmo and the import; each file is listed on `src/src.md`.
- `tests` — one plain C program per module, found by the build; each is listed
  on `tests/tests.md`.
- `include/3d/projection.h` — a lens into a projection matrix, and a pose and a
  lens into the render view everything is drawn and picked with. Its header says
  why this is neither `math`'s nor `scene`'s, and which three files agree
  about depth running backwards, for the sun's box too.
- `include/3d/shadow_cascades.h` — the sun's four cascades fitted to a view on
  the CPU. Its header says how the splits fall, why a slice is bounded by a
  sphere and why its centre snaps to whole texels in double.
- `include/3d/bounce_casters.h` — where a target's bounce casters last stood,
  kept by the caller. Its header says why an entity's own rows cannot hold it,
  whose it is, and what past 512 loses.
- `include/3d/mesh_component.h` — what an entity draws, as a range in `render`'s
  pools, and which of the two layers it draws in. Its header says why it holds
  an id and not a buffer, why the layer belongs to the drawable, and why the
  geometry is the one field that changes after creation.
- `include/3d/panel_component.h` — what an entity draws when it draws a surface
  of elements: this frame's element range, its size in millimetres and its
  layer. Its header says why the range is one frame's, why a panel is a
  component and not an entity, and why there is no panel system.
- `include/3d/material_component.h` — the factors and texture ids a surface is
  shaded with, its alpha mode, whether it is lit, its repeat, and the record
  `render` holds and the values it is made and rewritten from.
- `include/3d/shape_component.h` — which built-in shape an entity draws, in
  what colour or wearing which `.material`, and the collider that fits each
  kind. Its header says why a shape survives a save, why a kind is one of a
  fixed set, why the colour is linear and when a material is worn.
- `include/3d/model_component.h` — which model file an entity wears, as a
  project-relative path, whether it blends with the ground and over what
  height, 0.05 to 10 m, and the intent and drain that change it. Its header says why a path
  and not an id, and why a part's material is the model's.
- `include/3d/shape_system.h` — the shapes' GPU side, and the run that gives a
  shape the mesh and material it draws with. The file says why the upload is a
  startup operation, what the capacity constants size a device with, why the run
  is idempotent, and how an unknown kind is reported.
- `include/3d/shape_geometry.h` — the three built-in shapes' triangles and their
  surfaces' edges, kept on the CPU and keyed by kind. Its header says why a ray
  and a silhouette need geometry the card cannot be asked for, how the weld by
  position works, and that the arena is the caller's.
- `include/3d/pick.h` — what is under this pixel: the ray a render view sends
  through a point of its picture, and the frontmost entity it meets, lamps
  among them, and where a landscape's heights are met. Its header says why the
  question is this folder's and how a hit is measured.
- `include/3d/bounds.h` — an entity tree's size as the sphere round its shapes'
  and loaded models' world box, a landscape's heights' box among them, and the distance that frames it. Its header
  says what has a size, why a sphere and how far out it stays exact.
- `include/3d/outline.h` — one entity's silhouette, seen from one camera, as
  quads about the eye. The file says what a silhouette edge is, why the quads
  stand about the eye rather than on the screen, why the width is per
  vertex, why the edge count is a cap, and whose arena it all is.
- `include/3d/camera_marker.h` — a scene camera drawn in an editor's view: its
  box and frustum as line quads, and the ray that picks its box. Its header says
  where the geometry comes from, whose quads they are and why only the box is hit.
- `include/3d/sun_marker.h` — a sun drawn in an editor's view: a circle and an
  arrow along the way it shines as line quads, and the ray that picks its cube.
  Its header says why lines, why scale is ignored and why a cube is hit.
- `include/3d/point_light_marker.h` — a point light drawn in an editor's view:
  three wire circles about its position as line quads, and the ray that picks
  its cube. Its header says why a ball, why world axes and why a cube is hit.
- `include/3d/place_marker.h` — a place with no mesh drawn in an editor's view:
  a wire diamond about its position as line quads, the ray that picks its cube,
  and who wears it. Its header says why a diamond, why world axes, why a cube is
  hit and why the draw and the pick ask one predicate.
- `include/3d/brush_marker.h` — a sculpting brush drawn in an editor's view:
  two rings on a landscape's ground as line quads. Its header says why lines on
  the ground and why two rings.
- `include/3d/collider_marker.h` — a collider drawn as line quads about the eye:
  a box's edges, a sphere's circles, a capsule's circles, lines and arcs. Its
  header says why lines and not a solid, and whose shape it draws.
- `include/3d/gizmo.h` — the move gizmo as arithmetic: its size, every gizmo's
  handles and the kind a pass draws (arrows, rings or scales), the handle a ray
  meets, the grab and the triangles. Its header says why one call answers what
  is drawn and grabbed.
- `include/3d/gizmo_rings.h` — the rotate gizmo as arithmetic: three rings about
  the world axes, the ring a ray meets, the angle it points at and this frame's
  triangles. Its header says why world axes, why an angle and not a delta, and
  why a ring is quads facing the eye.
- `include/3d/gizmo_scales.h` — the scale gizmo as arithmetic on the entity's
  own axes: the handle a ray meets, the grab, the factor a drag comes to and
  this frame's triangles. Its header says why own axes, why from the press and
  why a floor.
- `include/3d/gizmo_snap.h` — snap as arithmetic: a position's dragged world axes,
  a swept angle and a scale's components to the nearest step. Its header says why
  the world grid, why turns are steps from the press, why a scale never reaches
  nought and that the Inspector never calls it.
- `include/3d/normal_matrix.h` — a world matrix into the matrix its normals
  want. Its header says why a normal cannot be carried by the world matrix, what
  that looks like on screen, and why this is neither `math`'s nor `render`'s.
- `include/3d/depth_sort.h` — the order see-through objects have to be drawn
  in. Its header says why blending is not commutative, why the key is one point
  per object, and which way round "further away" is.
- `include/3d/draw_system.h` — the shadow passes and bounce, the lights,
  blockers, sky, Haze, picture, Clouds and Fog volume rows, foliage gathered once a
  view, a shadowless kind's for the camera only, then the world and an editor's
  marks, the gizmo of its kind, drawn into the loop's pass.
- `include/3d/light_blocker.h` — a light blocker's box in the world at a lag,
  as a physics shape. Its header says what the box is, why the pass and the
  editor's lines for every blocker share it, and why a physics shape.
- `include/3d/fog_volume.h` — a fog volume's box at a lag, and a world's fog
  volumes as a pass's records about an eye. Its header says why the pass and the
  editor's lines share the box, why the density is squared and why the fields
  wrap in double.
- `include/3d/import.h` — a `.glb`'s bytes into entities. Its header says which
  folder each step of the pipeline belongs to, where the node tree is flattened,
  and the one thing decomposing a world matrix loses.
- `include/3d/models.h` — the model store: each model file loaded once by path,
  baked into parts wearing what its parts file names, or kept as failed; a
  landscape as heights and paint textures on a shared grid; a `.material` as
  one part with no geometry, its values rewritten in a frame.
- `include/3d/model_prepare.h` — a read `.glb` prepared for the store on any
  thread: level 0, its levels, shape and sphere baked, the put left to the
  device's thread. Its header says where the split falls and why the model is
  kept by pointer.
- `include/3d/models_foliage.h` — the store's foliage: copies scattered, erased
  and put back, kinds, looks and growth set, placed transforms and grown
  copies. Its header says the room, why placed transforms are kept, and why
  grown copies are the material's and not saved.
- `include/3d/model_levels.h` — a `.glb`'s levels file: the levels made as its
  parts file asks or its own measured, the bytes they are kept in, the read
  that calls a file stale, each level's triangles, and the level a view draws.
- `include/3d/landscape.h` — a landscape's heights met by a point and a ray,
  stamped by a brush, and its nodes chosen by the eye's distance from a min/max
  pyramid; its brush kinds name Scatter and Erase, which are foliage.h's.
- `include/3d/foliage.h` — a landscape's foliage copies placed on its ground
  at their kind's base size, scattered and erased by a brush, put back by undo and placed again after a
  sculpt. Its header says why the spacing stops a held brush near the density
  and why erase keeps no order.
- `include/3d/foliage_grow.h` — the copies a landscape's layers grow over an area, remade
  from the paint on a hashed lattice with no state. Its header says why no state, why the
  lattice is at the most any layer asks, the cost and the cap.
