# examples

Example projects the editor opens, each a folder with its own scene and code.

- `capsule` — a capsule that walks and jumps through a walled level of every kind of collision.
- `coin_game` — the coin game: collect the coins before the score runs out.
- `sun_and_moon` — a sun and a moon that trade day for night, and a cave lit by its own light.
- `tank_game` — the top-down tank game, grown milestone by milestone.
