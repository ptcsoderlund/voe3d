# capsule

A project for the editor: a capsule with a kinematic body that W/A/S/D walks and Space jumps,
a camera that follows it, and a walled level that shows every kind of collision.

- `main.scene` — the level: a 2 m gap in the floor to fall through, walls to stop and slide
  along, a gentle 15° ramp to climb, a steep 55° one that is not, a 0.25 m ledge to step onto,
  and five trigger coins.
- `Code` — the project's components and systems; each file is listed on `Code/Code.md`.
