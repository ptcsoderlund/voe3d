# sun_and_moon

A project for the editor: a sun and a moon, two directional lights that each cast their own
shadows and trade day for night as it plays, and a cave lit by a light of its own.

- `main.scene` — the scene: a ground with three boxes, a white Sun from high on +x and a faint
  blue Moon from low on −x, each with Day Night, and a Cave whose Block All blocker holds Cave Light.
- `Code` — the project's components and systems; each file is listed on `Code/Code.md`.
