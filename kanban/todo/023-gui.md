> **THIN AND PROVISIONAL, and the thinnest of the three.** Written far ahead at the
> principal's request. This one is a scope sketch only — a GUI is several cards, and
> which several is not knowable yet.

## Goal

A GUI that can be drawn in the world or in front of the camera, on a quad.

## Why this was planned for from the very beginning

**This is the case ADR-0024 was written to protect.** A GUI mapped onto a surface
inside the 3D world is the reason nothing in this engine gets a flat screen-space
path, and it is the reason the frame became an offscreen target early. Normally GUI
comes first and 3D is bolted on; here it is deliberately the other way round.

## The real question, and it needs a decision before any card

**How does a UI quad sit above the world without a separate rendering path?**
Everything obeys the depth buffer and reverse-Z (ADR-0033), so "on top" has to be
achieved with depth and passes rather than with a second renderer. That is a
decision, it wants an ADR, and it should be taken before this card is written
properly. Raised in the register when ADR-0049 was accepted.

## Rough scope, in no committed order

- Panels as quads — in world, or locked to the camera.
- Rendering widgets into a texture, which is the *panel* mechanism from card 021
  rather than the atlas one.
- Hit testing, which needs a ray from the pointer into the world — so it needs card
  016's input and the camera, and it is genuinely 3D picking rather than 2D
  coordinate comparison. This is the part that is easy to underestimate.
- Whether the layout model is retained or immediate. A real decision with an ADR,
  not an implementation detail.

## Not in scope, and worth saying now

The editor. It is wanted (ADR-0009) and it is what a GUI eventually serves, but it
is not this card and it is not the next one either.
