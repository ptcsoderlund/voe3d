# tests

`game`'s own tests: plain C programs, zero for pass, found by the build.

- `world.c` — each of the twenty-nine public keys, the picture's, the clouds' and the fog volume's among them, resolves on a fresh world, to twenty-nine different types, and the world counts thirty-one, the shape changes table once and runtime-only; its keys come through `game/scene.h`.
- `project.c` — a project type's menu, default, replaces on a game world, its need named by a call and refused when wrong, and a two-entity prefab spawned, refused by unknown name, removed and repeated a thousand times without a leak.
- `steps.c` — the fixed steps and their lag on a game world, and what each step moves: bodies, followers, emitters, sounds, water, a point light's replace, as a fade sends it, and the clouds' and fog's drift.
- `frame.c` — headless game frames of a camera, lights and shapes, each case's world drawn true or read back, from shadows and blockers to a sky, an interface, a missing model and the view's counts, a haze replace, a clouds row and a fog volume reaching their records; skips without a graphics card.
- `interface.c` — a 1280×720 surface is 240×135 mm; a run hands back a stand-in interface's answer and the asks it set, and a panel with a label and a button leaves element records. Skips without a graphics card.
- `starting.c` — the starting frame, plain and with a splash in a wide and a tall window, read back, the wait around the shaders step leaving it prepared with its cache written and a refusing work's wait false, and the progress and loaded lines. Skips without a graphics card.
- `models.c` — the model loader on real files: models, pictures, water, progress, landscapes, materials, a broken map's path kept, layers, parts, foliage kinds and copies at their base size, and a row's landscape's kinds in one update; skips without a graphics card.
- `models_levels.c` — a `.glb`'s levels file and the `.parts` count and swaps it is made with, prepared and reloaded, its swaps patched without a remake, its triangle counts per level, and a broken `.glb` kept failed with no levels file; skips without a graphics card.
- `models_cooked.c` — a `.glb`'s and a map's cooked copies, BC7 or BC5: written by the loader's calls, kept when fresh, made again on a change, trusted by the game's, queued, written by the stepped queue, shown as progress, counted stale and cooked by the editor's check; skips without a card.
- `models_batch.c` — three two-picture `.glb`s cooked in one batch of bursts under a splash match prepare's one at a time, `a_batchs_first_error_is_its_first_failing_file` and `a_dressed_picture_is_a_white_texel`; skips without a card.
- `compressing.c` — the compressing queue on a headless device against PNGs it writes: a job stepped until its BC7 copy is written at its stamp, a second add replacing the first, clear dropping every job, a missing map writing nothing, a normal map BC5; skips without a card.
- `glb_build.h` — the hand-built one-triangle `.glb` and the world helpers the four model tests include.
